The canvas arranges itself top to bottom as you build. Click a block to edit it, or drag one to put it where you want — Tidy up hands the arranging back. To connect two blocks, click the dot or the branch label a connector should leave from, then click the block it should lead to. Hover a connector for its × and its two end handles: × deletes it, and dragging an end onto another block (or another branch label) moves it there. A dashed line is a Goto block's jump — change it by editing that block.

To move several blocks at once, drag a box on empty canvas, or Ctrl-click blocks to add and remove them one at a time. Ctrl+A selects everything and Escape clears it. Then drag any selected block and the whole selection moves together, connectors included. Selecting a connector picks up the two blocks it joins, so dragging it moves both.

A connector that runs somewhere awkward can be routed by hand: drag the line itself and it bends through the point you drop it at, up to four bends per connector. Drop a bend back onto the straight line to remove it, double-click a connector to straighten it completely, and note that Tidy up asks before throwing hand-routing away along with your arrangement.
