{# The three views of Integrations: the apps you can connect, the connections you have, and the workflows that move records between them. **Links, not Bootstrap's JavaScript tabs.** Each tab is a page somebody can bookmark, send to a colleague and land on directly, and each already has its own route and its own HTMX targets. Loading all three into one document to switch them in the browser would mean three tables' worth of markup on every visit and one broken script away from a page with nothing on it. `tab` is set by the route rather than derived from the URL here, because the route is what knows: a template guessing from a path is a template that quietly highlights the wrong tab the day a path changes. #}
