A connection is one authenticated relationship with one external system — a store, a CRM account, an API. You can have as many as you need of the same kind: three Shopify stores and forty locations are the ordinary case, not an edge case. Credentials are encrypted before they are stored and are never sent back to this page: an edit form starts empty, and leaving it empty means "leave the stored one alone". Test makes one real call and shows you what came back, because a connection that saves is not the same as one that works.
